Before You Ask

Questions worth answering upfront

The things every client asks before signing — answered before you have to.

Why do retainers need 100% advance payment?+

Retainers are billed monthly, in advance, so the team can start work on day one of the cycle instead of chasing invoices mid-sprint — standard practice for a lean agency that can't carry receivables. If you'd rather test the relationship first, the Audit is the lowest-risk way to do that before any retainer starts.

Why is ad spend billed separately from your fee?+

Every rupee of ad spend goes directly on your own card or ad account, never ours. That means you keep full ownership of your ad account, pixel data and spend history, and can see exactly where the money goes in your own dashboard, not ours.

What's the minimum commitment?+

Retainers run on a 3-month minimum. Paid campaigns and content systems need at least one full optimization cycle to show a real signal — a shorter window rarely gives an honest read either way, in your favor or ours.

Is GST included in the prices shown?+

Prices shown are exclusive of GST where we're registered — it's added to the invoice as a line item, never hidden inside the quoted number.

What happens if a campaign isn't performing?+

You see the real numbers — ad spend versus results, reviewed line by line in strategy calls. If a channel isn't working, we say so and reallocate rather than keep billing against it quietly.

What exactly is the ChatGPT Ads add-on?+

A new ad channel, live in India since late August 2026, with no reliable benchmarks yet. We run it as a measured test against a defined budget (platform minimum ₹725/day) and report the real numbers back — never a promised outcome.
